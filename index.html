01 · about

Fresh out of the pipeline, already shipping

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah, majoring in Software Engineering. Most of my university and internship work sits at the backend and database layer — building the systems other people's screens quietly depend on.

My final year project combined video steganography with machine learning: hiding data inside video frames using LSB encoding, then using motion tracking and classifiers (Random Forest, Decision Tree, SVM) to keep the hidden signal secure without breaking the footage. That mix of security thinking and applied ML is the direction I want to keep building in.

Outside of coursework, I've spent time on the factory floor as much as in the IDE — building barcode systems and inventory tools that had to work reliably for people who weren't developers themselves.
